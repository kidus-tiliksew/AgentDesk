## Not available in this harness
The live NSPopover and its will-show and did-show events are not captured: the harness opens no window on screen. The scroll section above drives the shipped list in a retained hosting controller moved between offscreen windows, and calls the requests AppState makes on those events.
The scroller under a live change of the Show scroll bars setting is not captured; the scroll section above posts the change notification instead.
AXIsProcessTrusted=true: live VoiceOver output and the live NSPopover open and close are not captured; row accessibility strings above come from DecisionRowModel, which DecisionRow applies as its summary element's label and value, with Reject, Approve, and the expanded controls as separate elements.
Keys are not captured: the popover's key monitor needs the live popover window as key window. PopoverKeysTests covers the key-to-action mapping; Escape with no row expanded closes the popover.
Live notifications are not captured: UNUserNotificationCenter needs the app bundle and the user's permission. The permission prompt, a delivered banner, a click opening the popover, the Reject and Approve buttons, and removal from Notification Center are checked on a Mac before each release; the content, category, and Notifier timeline above use the shipped code with a fixture center.
