# Mac app evidence

`run.sh` produces retained evidence for the menu bar label, the popover, notifications, the built bundle's update settings and app icon, and the release script, without an account or any macOS permission. It needs the network to resolve the pinned Sparkle package and to read the `origin` remote's tags. It writes to `output/`, or to the directory given as its first argument:

```sh
apps/macos/Evidence/run.sh
```

It runs eight steps and records each command, exit status, and log in `output/manifest.json`:

1. Builds `Harness/` together with every `AgentDeskKit` source and the shipped `DecisionRow`, `AnswerControls`, `DecisionDetailView`, `AgentDetailView`, `DetailPanelWindow`, `PopoverKeyObserver`, `AgentsView`, `ScrollSupport`, `Ink`, `MenuBarLabel`, `NotificationController`, and `NotificationPauseLine` files into one program (`build.log`).
2. Runs the harness (`harness.log`, `report.txt`, PNGs):
   - the popover from fixtures, in light and dark, with the agents list expanded and collapsed, and empty;
   - each row marker (`popover-markers`): the new row's dot and heavier title, the Urgent marker, red time left, and "Suggests <answer>" on approve-or-reject rows only, with the short agent name;
   - an 18-row list overflowing with its scroller (`popover-overflow`), with the Undo strip (`popover-overflow-undo`);
   - the agents line and list (`popover-agents-line`, `popover-agents-quiet-hidden`, `popover-agents-quiet-revealed`), 18 sessions overflowing the agents list (`popover-agents-overflow`), working, waiting, blocked, quiet, done, and no-status sessions with default, nested, custom, and long names from the list's top and end (`popover-agents-states`, `popover-agents-states-end`), and every such entry without the 160 pt limit (`agents-entries`);
   - the shipped row and entry measured from their pixels on a flat fill: the row's padding and spacing, the 6 pt dot in the leading margin, one title edge for new and seen rows, each entry's dot color, one line, and padding, color only on the dot, a long name leaving the state whole, and the agents line's padding;
   - the list across openings, with one retained hosting controller moved between offscreen windows as `NSPopover` keeps its content view: a first opening, a reopening after scrolling without the top reset (the defect) and with it, an urgent row arriving above a kept offset, a read landing at the top, the Undo strip and the expanded agents list within 560 pt, a notification's row winning over the top reset, a repeated row target, a selection change, and each overflowing list's scroller, including after the Show scroll bars change notification (`popover-open-first`, `popover-reopen-no-reset`, `popover-reopen-reset`, `popover-urgent-above-offset-no-reset`);
   - the default menu bar style: count when unset, and a stored dot staying dot;
   - the detail panel, in light and dark, from the shipped `DecisionDetailView`: each of the five input types with its Markdown body, facts, and controls (`panel-approve-reject`, `panel-single-choice`, `panel-multiple-choice`, `panel-free-text`, `panel-single-choice-or-text`); a long decision scrolling at the 560 pt limit with its scroller (`panel-long`); parent and follow-up links (`panel-linked`); each recorded outcome with no controls (`panel-settled-*`); a failed send, a failed refresh, a held answer, loading, an unavailable decision, and a failed load; and the popover and the panel side by side (`panel-beside-popover`);
   - the agent panel, in light and dark, from the shipped `AgentDetailView`: a working, a stale, and a blocked session (`agent-working`, `agent-stale`, `agent-blocked`), a session with no status (`agent-no-status`), a `javascript:` and a relative link offering no control (`agent-invalid-link`, `agent-relative-link`), a 200-character line with 20 history entries scrolling at the 560 pt limit (`agent-long`), a failed refresh, loading and a failed load with and without the list's record, a pending list not read yet (`agent-pending-unread`), a session that is gone (`agent-gone`), and the popover beside the panel (`agent-beside-popover`), with each scene's model fields and AppKit controls;
   - each panel scene's AppKit controls, which show that a settled, unavailable, or held decision has only the close control, what each input type's untouched `AnswerDraft` submits, and each recorded outcome's text;
   - the paired windows: a real `NSPopover` beside the shipped `DetailPanelController` and `PairedWindowLifetime`, with the shipped `PopoverKeyObserver` in both windows, driven by events posted to the process. It checks the panel's width, side, and top at both ends of the screen, a click in the panel and in the popover keeping both, a click on the item's window and in an open menu keeping both, a click in another window, a posted deactivation, and a posted Space change closing both, closing only the panel leaving the popover's events alone, one will-show and one did-close per opening, each key from either window, text fields keeping ↓ and ↩, two-step Escape, and the Tab stops the system's key view loop reaches. Every window in this section is fully transparent, the panel is shown without taking key focus, and a run fails if the process ever becomes the active app, so nothing shows and focus stays with the user's work;
   - the agent panel in the same transparent paired windows, through the shipped `DecisionCoordinator`: the panel's frame, entries replacing each other, a pending decision and a session replacing each other without the panel hiding, opening the shown session, a click in either window, a click elsewhere, a posted deactivation, ⌘↩ sending nothing on a session and no selected row in its place, ↩, and two-step Escape;
   - the Undo strip, a conflict's recorded outcome, and a row restored with "Couldn't send. Try again.";
   - linked recovery, through the shipped `DecisionCoordinator` with a fixture sender, reader, and clock: a pending decision absent from the list held and undone with no read or send, then a failed send, a failed list read, a retained-snapshot publish, a new read with a failed direct read, and a retry that posts once (`popover-linked-held`, `popover-linked-send-failed`, `panel-linked-send-failed`);
   - each row's text, weight, red time left, and accessibility label and value, and the agents line;
   - the strip texts;
   - contrast of the popover inks, the dot inks, the title, and the system colors they replace, rendered as 40 pt swatches on the vibrant popover material and on a flat popover gray;
   - contrast of every shipped `Ink` value, Increase Contrast included, computed against the rendered backgrounds and pure white or black;
   - known solid fills read back through the same calls as the rendered contrast, which is reported and gates nothing;
   - the label as glyph, dot, a fade frame, and counts, and the seven fade frames;
   - a dot timeline through `DotPresence`: the fade on appearance, no redraw on refresh, and Reduce Motion;
   - the header with the pause line, in light and dark;
   - notification content and the `decision.approve_reject` category from the shipped `NotificationController`, with the default sound and no user info;
   - a `Notifier` timeline on a fixture clock and notification center: authorization after sign-in, push against digest and mute, the 2-minute interactive hold, the 10-minute age limit, no post while open, pause, Off, a post in flight posted once, a refused post retried, removal when decisions leave the read, and a post landing after sign-out removed;
   - Reject and Approve through `PendingAnswers.sendNow`, posted once, leaving a held popover answer alone, and a 409 outcome kept for the next opening;
   - click focus through `NotificationFocus`: a listed decision, a click during a refresh, and a stale target.
   The harness exits non-zero when a popover exceeds 340 × 560 pt, a text ink falls below 4.5:1 or a green, amber, or indigo dot ink falls below 3:1 on any background it measures or computes, a label image is not a template, a row marker, row or entry measurement, agent dot, or agents group is wrong, the first row is not at the top after an ordinary opening, a row target does not win, an overflowing list has no visible scroller, a panel is not 400 pt wide or exceeds 560 pt, a settled decision has an answer control, or a paired-window, key, or linked-recovery check fails.
3. Runs the harness again in its own process as `--access -AppleKeyboardUIMode 2` (`access.log`, `access.txt`), for the panel's keyboard and assistive evidence. It posts Tab and Shift-Tab to the shipped panel in each of the five input types and names each stop, tries two process-only overrides of full keyboard access and records whether each took effect, reads the panel's accessibility tree through `AXUIElement` for each input type and state, checks that each control has a name, that each button, link, radio button, and checkbox offers `AXPress`, that pressing a selection of them gives the expected result (Reject, Approve, Send, one radio button, one checkbox, each link, Try Again, and the close control on each input type and settled scene), that a settled decision has only the close control, and that urgency, the time left, and a suggestion read as text. It reads the agent panel's scenes the same way, checks that what `req-mac-app` AC-11.3 lists reads as text, that Open link is the one control that opens a link, that a pending decision and each agent entry are named buttons that open no browser, that no element reads as a web link, and that no text field exists. It fails when any check fails or the process becomes the active app. It needs Accessibility trust for the tree: an untrusted process records that as not exercised.
4. Runs the full suite, `swift test --package-path apps/macos/AgentDeskKit`, as CI does (`tests.log`). `freshness.txt` holds the measured time from a server change to a published snapshot, open and closed.
5. Builds the app unsigned with the CI command, `xcodebuild build -project apps/macos/AgentDesk.xcodeproj -scheme AgentDesk -configuration Debug -destination 'platform=macOS' CODE_SIGNING_ALLOWED=NO`, into a scratch derived-data directory so every run compiles from scratch (`xcodebuild.log`).
6. Audits the bundle that build produced (`bundle.txt`), without launching it: the value and type of each Sparkle key in `Info.plist`, that `SUPublicEDKey` decodes to a 32-byte Ed25519 public key, that `Contents/Frameworks/Sparkle.framework` is version 2.10.1, that `LSUIElement` is true, the `AppIcon` set's ten slots at their pixel sizes, its 1024 pixel file equal to `apps/mobile/assets/icon.png`, the build setting in Debug and Release, `CFBundleIconName` and `CFBundleIconFile`, the compiled `AppIcon.icns` and `Assets.car`, and the icon `NSWorkspace` returns for the bundle (`IconAudit/`, `bundle-icon-finder.png`), compared with the generic application icon and the source. Any other value fails the step.
7. Checks the release script with `bash -n` on `release.sh` and `test-release.sh`, then runs `test-release.sh` (`release-tests.log`).
8. Runs `release.sh` dry runs in a throwaway clone of the commit, whose `origin` is this checkout's `origin` remote, because the outputs above leave this checkout dirty (`release-dry-run.log`). Each run states the exit it expects: a malformed version, an untracked file, and a `macos-v` tag created only in the clone are refused, and a valid version on the clean clone stops before archiving. A different exit fails the step.

The run fails when Git would ignore any output, so every file the manifest names is committed with it. The manifest names the Git tree hashes of `AgentDesk/`, `AgentDeskKit/`, `AgentDesk-Info.plist`, `AgentDesk.xcodeproj`, `scripts/`, `Harness/`, `IconAudit/`, and `run.sh` at the commit the run used, and the SHA-256 of every output. Compare them with `git rev-parse <commit>:apps/macos/AgentDesk` and the like to confirm the outputs belong to a later commit's sources. `sources_dirty` is true when those paths had uncommitted changes.

## Live checks: not exercised

Everything `run.sh` retains is non-live. Unit tests, offscreen renders, events posted to a real `NSPopover` in transparent windows, text-only Tab stops with Keyboard navigation off, and `AXUIElement` calls answered in-process do not establish live behaviour. The operator's pre-release checks on a Mac (`component-mac-app` §Verification) hold these, and none has passed, because none was exercised:

- the real status item position and the panel's side at both ends of the menu bar;
- physical clicks in the panel and the popover, and physical keys in both windows;
- a real click on the desktop and in another app, and a real deactivation;
- Tab and Shift-Tab through every panel control with Keyboard navigation on;
- VoiceOver speech and focus, including focus moving on opening, replacing, and closing the panel;
- the agent entries and the agent panel's controls through Tab and Shift-Tab with Keyboard navigation on, and through VoiceOver, and a click on Open link reaching the default browser.

Two live attempts on 2026-11-05 gathered no evidence of behaviour, and neither posted a click or a key. The first ran on a locked screen. The second stopped at its first row click, for a cause not established. The operator then chose to proceed without a live test. `req-mac-app` AC-11.11 is unchanged, and this evidence does not establish its reach.

## Limits

- Offscreen windows render the popover material without the desktop behind it, which is the closest available stand-in for the `NSPopover` window.
- The measured contrast figures read pixels through a bitmap path that does not return the color drawn: a solid `#2E2E2E` fill reads back as `#3C3C3C`, and a solid `#12703A` fill as about `#417F50`. The cause is not established. `report.txt` lists each known fill's read-back, so read the measured figures with that shift in mind. The computed list's `#3C3C3C` stays a gated background.
- `NSAppearance(named: .accessibilityHighContrastAqua)` resolves to plain Aqua offscreen, so Increase Contrast is computed from the shipped values, not rendered.
- The harness never launches a build, so the installed app's menu bar item, `PopoverController`, `AppState`, and physical clicks and keys are not captured, and a click in another app, which only the global monitor sees, is not captured: the harness leaves that monitor off, so what the Mac's user clicks during a run cannot fail it. The paired-window section stands in for them with the shipped lifetime, panel, and observer classes, a real `NSPopover`, posted events, a key-handling model that makes the same policy calls as `AppState`, and transparent windows. The operator's pre-release check covers the live app. The scroll section drives the shipped list in a retained hosting controller moved between offscreen windows and calls the requests `AppState` makes on those events. Offscreen windows are occluded, so an animated scroll never advances and the row target is applied without its animation. A fresh process's first layout stays at the top there, so the clipped opening is reproduced from a kept offset, not from a first show. `ListScrollRequestTests` covers the request policy, and the operator's pre-release check covers the live popover.
- `NSWorkspace` returns the built bundle's icon as Finder reads it. The icon in System Settings and in a delivered notification, which macOS resolves through the registered app for the bundle id, needs an installed build, so it is not captured.
- The `--access` step fails intermittently: in some runs the `AXUIElement` read of a scene returns nothing or a tree of nested `AXApplication` elements, and that scene's checks fail. The cause is not established. On 2026-11-06 it failed 1 of 22 scratch runs at the base commit `18ed7b7` and 3 of 19 scratch runs of the harness that added the dot gates, which does not show whether that harness changed the rate. A run with a failure exits non-zero, and only a run where every step passes is retained.
- `access.txt` shows what an assistive client is given, through `AXUIElement` calls answered inside the harness's process. It does not show what VoiceOver speaks, and VoiceOver never starts.
- The panel's Tab stops are read with the Mac's Keyboard navigation setting, which the harness never changes, so with that setting off they hold the text fields only. Neither process-only override reproduces the setting on, so buttons, links, radio buttons, and checkboxes in the Tab order are not captured. Run the harness with the setting on to read them.
- Keys from a physical keyboard are not captured. The paired-window section posts them to the shipped observers, and `PopoverKeysTests` covers what each key does; Escape with no panel open closes the popover.
- `UNUserNotificationCenter` needs the app bundle and the user's permission, so the permission prompt, a delivered banner, a click opening the popover, the notification buttons, and removal from Notification Center are not captured. The harness drives the shipped planning and content code through a fixture center instead.
- Offscreen windows are never key, so buttons draw in their inactive style and the accent comes from the system, not the app's asset catalog.
- The freshness measurement uses an in-process `URLProtocol` stub, so it bounds the app's own share of the delay. A request may take up to the 15-second timeout on a slow network.
